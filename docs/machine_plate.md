### Materials

General:
- Berlinsans Roman font
- Super glue
- Black sharpie (optional)

Per plate:
- 8, 5x3mm (diameter, width) magnets. I bought the cheapest set of 100 I could find for ~£4.
- 3mm thick acrylic of your desired colour.

### Manufacture

First, you may wish to make a little calibration jig
to work out the optimal hole size for the magnets & laser you are using.
You can then adjust the design accordingly.

To lasercut these you should save them as a DXF
and import into whatever lasercutting software.

In your lasercutting software, you should delete / disable paths for numbers you aren't cutting.
(If you are colour coding them)

You should set the number to 'fill' or 'engrave'
(depending whatever terminology the software uses),
have that layer run first.

Tweak power & speed settings for engrave & cut for whatever your lasercutter recommends / works.

### Assembly

The magnets should press fit in, I occasionally had to use a hammer.
The polarity shouldn't matter but I like to keep them all the same.

I used 2 magnets per corner so would slide around less, as my magnets were very cheap.

Super glue the corner pieces onto the front plate.

Sharpie the engraved numbers for better contrast.
